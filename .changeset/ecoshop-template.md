Add the `ecoshop` template (`ecopages init <dir> --template ecoshop`), a Shopify storefront on Ecopages JSX and Radiant UI. It reads the catalog and cart from the Storefront API, or from a bundled demo catalog when no Shopify credentials are set, and its cart works without JavaScript. A verified Shopify webhook revalidates catalog pages by cache tag, and agents get an MCP server at `/mcp`, `/llms.txt`, Markdown product views and schema.org Product JSON-LD.
