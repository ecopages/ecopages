Publish `@ecopages/codemod` to npm with a CLI: run `npx @ecopages/codemod <transform>` from a project to migrate it, with `--list` to see the transforms and jscodeshift flags such as `--dry --print` passed through. The transforms are also importable as `@ecopages/codemod/<transform>`.
