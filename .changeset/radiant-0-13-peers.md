`@ecopages/ecopages-jsx` now requires `@ecopages/jsx` and `@ecopages/radiant` `^0.13.0` as peer dependencies, and `@ecopages/image-processor` accepts `@ecopages/jsx` `^0.13.0` as its optional peer, up from 0.11. Upgrade `@ecopages/jsx`, `@ecopages/radiant` and `@ecopages/signals` to 0.13, and `@ecopages/radiant-ui` to 0.7, with this release.
