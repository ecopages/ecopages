The `@ecopages/jsx`, `@ecopages/radiant` and `@ecopages/signals` peer and dependency ranges move to `^0.9.0` (`@ecopages/radiant-ui` `^0.4.0` and `@ecopages/radiant-themes` `^0.4.0` in the templates). Update them in your app with `pnpm add @ecopages/jsx@^0.9.0 @ecopages/radiant@^0.9.0 @ecopages/signals@^0.9.0`.
